Summer 2026 Internship

Frisco Transit Center Headquarters

Project Engineer Intern — MTech Mechanical

Project Summary

This summer, I interned as a project engineer for MTech Mechanical, a mechanical subcontractor. During this internship, I worked closely on the completion and startup phase of the Frisco Transit Center Headquarters in Frisco, Colorado.

Because this was a BABAA/FTA compliant project, the work involved more than just getting mechanical systems installed and running. Every piece of equipment and material had to be tracked, documented, and verified against federal requirements, which gave me a close look at how the office side of a project supports the work happening in the field.

Tools Used:
Procore Bluebeam Viewpoint Egnyte SharePoint

What I Learned

Procurement

I learned how equipment and materials move from the drawings to the job site. This included tracking orders, following up with suppliers, and making sure what was purchased matched what was specified and approved.

RFIs & Submittals

I created RFI and submittal documents, which taught me how to clearly communicate questions and product information to the general contractor and design team so that issues could be resolved without slowing down the work.

BABAA & FTA Compliance

Since this project was federally funded, I familiarized myself with the Build America, Buy America Act (BABAA) and FTA legislation. Materials and equipment needed documentation showing they met domestic content requirements, which added another layer to both procurement and submittals.

Field & Design Limitations

Working alongside the field showed me the design limitations for mechanical systems presented by the job site and the laborers installing them. What works on paper does not always work in the field, and understanding that is key to designing systems that can actually be built.

Software & Document Management

Throughout the internship, I familiarized myself with Procore, Egnyte, SharePoint, Bluebeam, and Viewpoint. These platforms were used daily to manage drawings, submittals, RFIs, and project documents, and to keep the office and the field working off the same information.
